# Errow Narrative Foundation

Status: **CANON source**

This document establishes the minimum fictional premise for Errow. It intentionally leaves the deeper cosmology unresolved.

## Core premise

### CANON — The Flow

Everything in Errow has a direction.

The underlying order that gives direction coherence is known as **the Flow**. Direction is not merely a UI convention inside the fiction; it is a fundamental property of the world.

An arrow naturally seeks to complete its direction. A blocked or contradictory arrangement is a disruption of that order.

### CANON — The Signal

A phenomenon known as **the Signal** is bound to the world's directional order.

At some point before the player's present experience, the Signal **broke**.

After the break, directions that should have remained coherent began to collide, interlock and form impossible arrangements.

### CANON — The player's act

A solved puzzle represents a local restoration of coherent Flow.

When the player finds the correct sequence and releases the arrows, the contradiction in that arrangement is resolved.

The mechanical loop and the fictional loop are therefore the same action:

**read direction -> resolve interference -> restore Flow**

### CANON — Foundational line

> There was once a direction for everything. Then the Signal broke.
>
> The arrows remember where they must go.
> They just don't remember why.

This line is authoritative as the foundational expression of Errow's fiction. Its language is deliberately poetic and does not, by itself, establish biological consciousness or literal memory.

## OPEN — Deliberately unresolved

The following are not canonically answered yet:

- What the Flow ultimately is: physical law, network, intelligence, metaphysical order or something else.
- What the Signal ultimately is and how it relates to the Flow.
- Who or what broke the Signal.
- Whether the break was accidental, intentional, cyclical or inevitable.
- Whether arrows are conscious entities, symbolic fragments, data-like signals, living beings or another form of existence.
- Who or what the player represents inside the fiction.
- Whether there was a civilization before the break.
- Whether different regions/worlds perceive the Flow differently.
- Whether restoring local Flow repairs the Signal or merely treats its symptoms.
- Whether the Signal can communicate.

These questions must remain OPEN until a later lore wave explicitly resolves them.

## Narrative constraints

Future lore should preserve these invariants unless an explicit canon revision is made:

1. The puzzle must remain understandable without story exposition.
2. Lore should deepen the meaning of direction, blockage, sequence, escape, interference and restoration rather than sit beside the mechanics as an unrelated plot.
3. Mystery is part of the identity. Do not explain the entire cosmology early.
4. Gameplay labels, level numbers and community-created content are not automatically canon.
5. New fictional assertions must be marked intentionally as CANON, RUMOR or OPEN.
6. The fiction should remain compatible with mobile, Web and short-session play: narrative discovery may reward progression but must not obstruct immediate puzzle play.

## Current narrative identity

Errow is an immediate arrow puzzle on the surface and a mystery about broken direction underneath.

Short-form positioning:

**Everything has a direction. Something broke the Signal. Restore the Flow.**
